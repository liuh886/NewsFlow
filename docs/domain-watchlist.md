# Domain and company watchlist

Updated: 2026-08-03 (Asia/Shanghai)

This document is a research baseline, not a list of automatically accepted claims. Company pages are `corporate_primary`: they are authoritative for the company's own filings, agreements, project stages and reported operating data, but they are not independent proof of industry leadership, project economics or wider market impact.

CCUS associations, technical programmes, standards bodies and their recurring reports are maintained separately in `docs/ccus-report-watchlist.md` so that company disclosures and institutional analysis retain distinct evidence rules.

## AI infrastructure

AI coverage follows the Energy → Chips → Infrastructure → Models → Applications taxonomy in `docs/ai-five-layer-watchlist.md`. Energy is a necessary base layer, not a synonym for the whole field.

### NVIDIA — accelerated compute, networking and AI-factory architecture

Watch:

- Data Center revenue, product transitions and supply constraints;
- rack-scale compute, networking and system architecture;
- partner deployments expressed in MW/GW, and the difference between intended and commissioned capacity;
- token throughput per watt or per megawatt, utilization and time to production.

Current official baseline:

- NVIDIA reported fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter Data Center revenue of $62.3 billion.
- The DSX platform announced in May 2026 extends NVIDIA's scope from chips into reference designs covering compute, networking, storage, power, cooling, controls and facility design.
- Its partnership with IREN describes an intention to support up to 5 GW over time; this is a target and partnership scope, not delivered capacity.

Official sources:

- https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/dsx-infrastructure-ai-factory

### TSMC — advanced logic manufacturing and packaging

Watch:

- leading-node production ramp, yield and customer mix;
- CoWoS and other advanced-packaging capacity and delivery;
- capital expenditure translated into usable wafer and packaging output;
- geographic fab ramp, cost differences and supply-chain resilience.

Evidence boundary:

- process roadmaps and planned capacity are not delivered output;
- customer demand commentary must remain attributed to TSMC;
- monthly revenue may show aggregate demand but does not by itself identify AI unit shipments.

Official entry points:

- https://investor.tsmc.com/english
- https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q2

### AMD — accelerators, CPUs and rack-scale systems

Watch:

- Instinct accelerator production, shipments and customer deployment;
- EPYC, accelerator, networking and rack-level integration;
- ROCm maturity and its effect on usable workload coverage;
- contracted capacity versus installed and utilized capacity.

Evidence boundary:

- roadmap performance is not production performance;
- announced gigawatts or partnerships are not commissioned systems;
- vendor benchmarks require hardware, software, precision and workload context.

Official entry point:

- https://ir.amd.com/

### Broadcom — custom silicon and AI networking

Watch:

- custom accelerator design wins and their production timing;
- scale-up and scale-out networking silicon and systems;
- AI revenue, customer concentration and supply commitments;
- whether networking capacity removes or shifts cluster bottlenecks.

Evidence boundary:

- design wins are not shipments;
- customer demand forecasts require attribution;
- component specifications do not establish end-to-end cluster performance.

Official entry point:

- https://investors.broadcom.com/

### Microsoft — hyperscale capacity, deployment speed and utilization

Watch:

- quarterly capital expenditure split between short-lived compute and long-lived facilities;
- capacity brought online, GPU dock-to-live time and continuing supply constraints;
- first-party accelerator deployment and inference throughput improvements;
- finance leases, power procurement and data-center project delivery.

Current official baseline:

- In its fiscal 2026 third-quarter call, Microsoft said it added another gigawatt of capacity in the quarter, reduced GPU dock-to-live time in its largest regions, and still expected capacity constraints through 2026.
- The company described planned 2026 calendar-year capital expenditure of roughly $190 billion. Treat this as management guidance, not completed investment.

Official source:

- https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2026/earnings-fy-2026-q3

### Alphabet — custom compute, data-center construction and energy integration

Watch:

- capital expenditure and its split between servers, data centers and networking;
- TPU/GPU supply, Cloud capacity and demand constraints;
- energy infrastructure ownership, acquisition and grid-connection strategy;
- depreciation and operating costs as evidence of infrastructure intensity.

Current official baseline:

- Alphabet's 2025 fourth-quarter call gave a 2026 capital-expenditure outlook of $175–185 billion.
- Management said approximately 60% of the comparable infrastructure investment mix was machines and 40% data centers and networking equipment.
- The announced Intersect transaction shows Alphabet moving closer to data-center energy infrastructure; until closing and integration, it remains a transaction milestone rather than delivered capacity.

Official sources:

- https://abc.xyz/investor/news/news-details/2025/Alphabet-Announces-Agreement-to-Acquire-Intersect-to-Advance-U-S--Energy-Innovation-2025-DVIuVDM9wW/default.aspx

### Meta — AI campuses, energy agreements and infrastructure financing

Watch:

- capital expenditure and new campus capacity;
- large-load tariffs, power agreements, transmission and water infrastructure;
- ownership, lease and joint-venture structures used to finance AI campuses;
- supply agreements for accelerators, CPUs, networking and fibre.

Current official baseline:

- Meta's 2025 full-year results gave a 2026 capital-expenditure range of $115–135 billion.
- In July 2026, Meta and BlackRock announced a financing venture for a 1 GW El Paso campus. The disclosed structure separates ownership and long-lived infrastructure financing from Meta's occupancy and operation; capacity is expected to begin coming online in 2028, so it must not be recorded as operating capacity today.

Official sources:

- https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/
- https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/meta-announces-new-venture-with-blackrock-to-develop-data-center-in-el-paso/

### Schneider Electric — power, cooling and facility reference designs

Watch:

- validated power and liquid-cooling designs for new rack architectures;
- rack power density, heat-removal assumptions, PUE and facility integration;
- production availability rather than partnership announcements alone;
- evidence from deployed facilities that confirms vendor design claims.

Current official baseline:

- In July 2026, Schneider Electric and AMD published a reference design for Helios deployments supporting racks up to 246 kW and modular clusters up to 10.4 MW IT load.
- These are engineering design specifications and modeled performance, not operating-fleet results.

Official source:

- https://www.se.com/ww/en/about-us/newsroom/news/press-releases/Schneider-Electric-and-AMD-release-first-Helios-platform-reference-design-to-accelerate-AI-Factory-deployment-6a60b540d574233932028c53/

## CCUS and energy transition

### Northern Lights — cross-border CO2 transport and storage

Watch:

- actual CO2 received, transported and injected;
- contracted versus operating capacity;
- new ships, wells, terminal capacity and anchor customers;
- Phase 2 construction, schedule and cross-border agreements.

Current official baseline:

- Northern Lights reported its first wastewater-derived CO2 injection in March 2026.
- Phase 2 is intended to expand annual transport and storage capacity from 1.5 million tonnes to more than 5 million tonnes by 2028; construction progress should be tracked separately from current operating capacity.

Official sources:

- https://norlights.com/newsroom/
- https://norlights.com/news/northern-lights-phase-2-expanding-europes-co%E2%82%82-storage-capacity/

### Heidelberg Materials — cement capture and product delivery

Watch:

- Brevik's actual capture rate, uptime, energy penalty and stored volume;
- customer delivery and accounting methodology for carbon-captured cement;
- Padeswood construction, funding conditions and commissioning schedule;
- transferability of Brevik operating experience to later projects.

Current official baseline:

- Heidelberg Materials describes Brevik CCS as opened and operating at industrial scale.
- Its 2026 results update says Padeswood has moved into implementation following a UK funding agreement. The next evidence threshold is construction and operating data, not another announcement.

Official source:

- https://www.heidelbergmaterials.com/en/pr-2026-02-25

### ExxonMobil Low Carbon Solutions — Gulf Coast transport and storage services

Watch:

- third-party contracts versus projects actually started;
- capture-source startups, transported and injected volumes;
- permits, storage development and use of the Denbury pipeline network;
- commercial structure for CCS-enabled power and data centers.

Current official baseline:

- ExxonMobil says its first commercial CCS operation started in 2025 and identifies three additional customer projects expected to start in 2026.
- It reports roughly 9 million tonnes per year under third-party contract. Both the schedule and contracted quantity remain company disclosures and require project-level confirmation.

Official sources:

- https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/news/viewpoints/2025-taking-carbon-capture-and-storage-from-momentum-to-impact
- https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/what-we-do/delivering-industrial-solutions/carbon-capture-and-storage

### SLB Capturi — industrial capture technology and project execution

Watch:

- FEED conversion into FID and construction contracts;
- design capture capacity versus actual annual captured volume;
- performance across cement, waste-to-energy, bioenergy and pulp applications;
- commissioning, handover and long-term operating support.

Current official baseline:

- SLB Capturi says the Brevik capture plant has operated since June 2025 with design capacity up to 400,000 tonnes per year.
- It reported more than 25 feasibility or pre-FEED studies and eight FEEDs in its first year. These indicate pipeline development, not completed projects.

Official source:

- https://www.slb.com/newsroom/updates/2025/20250916-capturi-update

## Promotion rules for company news

A company item may be automatically promoted only when:

1. the page is an official filing, earnings material, project release or technical document;
2. the summary says who is making the claim;
3. target, contracted, under construction, commissioned and operating are kept as distinct states;
4. every number has a claim-level citation;
5. evidence confidence above 4.5 includes independent non-corporate evidence;
6. the item changes a tracked project stage, capacity, cost, schedule, operating result or binding agreement.

Marketing claims, leadership claims, intentions without commitments and partnership announcements without measurable movement remain discovery material rather than formal Signals.
