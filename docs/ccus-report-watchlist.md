# CCUS institution and report watchlist

Updated: 2026-08-04 (Asia/Shanghai)

This is the recurring report channel for CCUS and energy-transition research. It complements the company watchlist; it does not replace project permits, government records, audited company disclosures or independent reporting.

## Current report families to monitor

- Global CCS Institute: [Global Status of CCS 2025](https://www.globalccsinstitute.com/global-status-of-ccs-2025/) and the [CCS policy, legal and regulatory snapshot](https://www.globalccsinstitute.com/ccsplrsnapshot/).
- IEAGHG: the continuously updated technical-report library, including 2026 work on cement decarbonisation, storage conformance, modular capture and cross-border CO2 transport.
- CCSA: [Annual Review 2025–26](https://www.ccsassociation.org/resources/ccsa-annual-review-2025-26/) plus delivery-plan and workforce updates.
- ZEP: the 2025 report [Designing Europe’s CO2 market and infrastructure](https://zeroemissionsplatform.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ZEP-report_CO2-transport-infrastructure.pdf).
- OGCI: the updated CCUS Hub Search and the [2025 Progress Report](https://www.ogci.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/251017_OGCI_ProgressReport2025_V21.2.pdf).
- Clean Energy Ministerial: the [January 2025 Global Overview of CCUS Policies and Programmes](https://www.cleanenergyministerial.org/content/uploads/2025/02/january-2025-global-overview-of-ccus-policies-and-programmes.pdf).
- ISO/TC 265: the catalogue of published and developing standards, especially geological storage, EOR storage, metering, ports and emissions monitoring.
- DNV: [Energy Transition Outlook: CCS to 2050](https://www.dnv.com/energy-transition-outlook/carbon-capture-storage/).

The list is a discovery baseline, not a freshness claim. Each run must recheck the official publication page and record what changed since the preceding report or Signal.

## Report evidence contract

Every report candidate must record:

- the report title, edition or version and verified publication date;
- the report's own data cutoff, not merely the webpage update date;
- whether the complete report and its methodology were reviewed;
- which statements are observed data, modeled estimates, scenarios or recommendations;
- the relevant geography, project-state definitions, capacity unit and time horizon;
- the institution's role and any membership, commercial or advocacy interest;
- the concrete delta against the preceding report or an existing Signal.

If a data cutoff is not disclosed, record `not_disclosed`; the candidate must go to `needs_review`. Paywalled or members-only text cannot support an automatic Signal unless the complete source has actually been accessed.

## Promotion rules

- A report may establish what its authors measured, modeled or recommended. It does not by itself establish that a project is financed, permitted, under construction, commissioned or operating.
- Project counts must be normalized to the same project-state taxonomy before comparison.
- Announced, prospective and modeled capacity must never be added to operating capacity.
- Forecasts must retain their base year, scenario, assumptions and range.
- Industry-association and coalition claims must be explicitly attributed and checked against government, regulator, project or independent records when they describe real-world delivery.
- A newer publication with unchanged underlying data is not a new Signal.
